# Completed work in ongoing chats

The original reader admitted only conversations created after learning was enabled, whose
creation-version matched the admitted reader and whose final turn was complete. That excluded
new work in long-running Codex chats. Passing fictional inference and native-delivery checks
did not establish working real intake.

The optional `completed-work-v1` policy requires a digest-bound owner confirmation under an
existing owner-authorized conversation grant. It reads only completed turns that started after
the existing grant and project cutoffs, completed at least 15 minutes ago, and remain within
the 30-day retention window. Earlier messages and unfinished turns are excluded. A conversation
can still supply only one supporting episode; repeated turns cannot manufacture independence.

The reader binary, generated schema, account and analysis model remain exactly admitted.
A conversation's recorded creation-version is metadata, rather than the binary currently
reading it. Delegated/forked/ephemeral conversations remain excluded. Native heartbeat and
environment wrappers are excluded; structured question replies contribute only their answers.
Owner-trusted requests are not relabeled as proven human authorship.

Normal project resolution remains bounded to registered repositories. An owner may separately
bind an exact conversation digest and expected workspace to one approved repository. This does
not grant its containing folder or other conversations. A changed binding, source policy or
grant fails closed. Activation resets pagination while retaining processed-conversation
checkpoints and all signed history. Its final confirmation is the commit marker, so an
interrupted policy write cannot open capture.

Owner-only desktop protocol operations are `autonomy.capture.preview`,
`autonomy.capture.enable` and `autonomy.capture.disable`. The agent context bridge remains
read-only. The policy adds no projects, categories, device permissions or repository-tools
grant. Native instructions and automatic-serving qualification remain separate controls.

Verify setup through the ordinary installed observer: distinguish authorized scope, actual
captured conversations, completed analysis, hypotheses, publication, delivery and helpfulness.
An empty profile is valid; status flags and fictional fixtures do not substitute for capture.
