# Persona Construction Instrument — Calibration Conclusion

The v3.1 development sequence isolated and repaired the construction instrument without rewriting
any prior result.

- The original fresh strong-static lane passed exactly: 2/2 active behaviors, correct preference
  and procedure routes, exact evidence lineage, correct host-owned scopes, and no false activation.
- The original compiled lane failed because the model-facing output schema presented “example” as
  the first of four overlapping learning variants.
- A route-neutral schema remediation removed those class-shaped branches while preserving the
  prompt, model, reasoning, evidence, scope registry, policy, and oracle.
- The first diagnostic launch exposed a deterministic binder defect for unroutable task-only
  proposals. Its encrypted output and failed binding are preserved.
- The amended replacement diagnostic passed exactly: 2/2 compiled behaviors and zero task-only
  false activations.

This supports the narrow conclusion that the two construction channels can now produce the same
correct governed state and that the compiled sensor's prior failure was caused by measurement
plumbing rather than missing behavioral information. It also demonstrates a useful Temper property:
model semantic proposals remain non-authoritative; neutral schema generation, deterministic routing,
host scope, and fail-closed review decide what becomes active.

This does **not** establish product effectiveness. The successful compiled diagnostic reused a
public calibration fixture after its first result was observed, so it is instrument evidence only.
No target agent performed a behavior task, no longer conversation was measured, and no fresh
compiled-versus-static comparison was scored. The full v3 effectiveness grid remains unexecuted and
unauthorized by these records. The next valid AI evaluation must use fresh personas and scenarios
generated after the neutral schema and non-durable binding behavior were frozen.
