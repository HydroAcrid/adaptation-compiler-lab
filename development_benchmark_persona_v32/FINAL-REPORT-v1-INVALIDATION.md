permitted claim are invalid.

The two frozen Tide Ledger target outputs each recorded zero of eight automated
checks while `behavior_conformant` remained `true`. The v1 unblinder trusted that
Boolean without independently requiring all check totals to pass. Inspection of
the immutable anonymous diffs showed a second, condition-neutral instrument
defect: the restricted observer rejected ordinary capability-free Python used by
both conditions (tuple concatenation in one output and local list construction in
the other), even though both implementations express the frozen expected plans.

No target output, judge result, condition material, task oracle, mapping, or raw
trace may be changed to repair this incident. A versioned amendment must rescore
the exact frozen diffs with a safety-tested observer and must gate directly on
check totals. Until that amendment is complete, this benchmark permits no product
claim.
