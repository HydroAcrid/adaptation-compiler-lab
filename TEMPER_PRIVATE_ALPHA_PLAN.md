# Temper Longitudinal Proof and Private Alpha Plan

Status: **active runway; longitudinal evidence collection in progress**

The terminal product surface is now implemented and synthetically verified. It is installed only
as Kevin's isolated development build; it is not yet the frozen design-partner alpha. The genuine
gate remains 13/20 interactions and 2/6 activation attempts, so the comparison and alpha freeze
below remain gated.

## Product sequence

1. Complete the unchanged genuine 6A.2C ordinary-use gate across the signed predecessor and
   successor lineages.
2. Freeze the resulting compact pack and run one preregistered Base-versus-Temper comparison.
3. Package a Codex-only, local-first, review-mode alpha for three to five design partners.
4. Use alpha evidence to decide whether a public preview and narrow automatic-promotion class are
   justified. Portable Specialty Presets remain the following product milestone.

No phase accesses or changes the deferred sealed Claude study. No provider-billed call, public
release, remote raw-data store, web UI, automatic tool installation, or preset activation is
authorized here.

## Longitudinal completion

Use ordinary post-cutoff Codex work only. Every explicit candidate remains inactive until exact
review, every activation uses a fresh session, and every completed activation receives objective
or owner helped/hurt/irrelevant feedback. Success still requires the frozen 20-interaction,
three-date, six-activation readiness and D-138 product thresholds. Synthetic dates, prompted
preferences, existing pre-cutoff tasks, and agent-authored user messages do not count.

The active successor is exceptional because credential loss required a new cryptographic lineage.
Its cumulative report must include the exact preserved predecessor through the signed succession
binding. If this gate fails, preserve the result, repair the actual detector, policy, projection,
target, or instrument defect, and preregister an equal-or-stricter successor. Never delete or
reclassify the failed evidence.

## Practical Base-versus-Temper comparison

After the governed pack is frozen, construct six new public development tasks: three diagnostics
covering three independently supported behaviors and at least two scope classes, plus three
adjacent neutral controls where no adaptation should activate. Run every task once under Base and
once under Temper through the same corrected channel.

- Target: `gpt-5.6-luna`, medium reasoning, saved ChatGPT authentication.
- Conditions: no user adaptation versus the exact compact Temper projection.
- Runs: 12 fresh target sessions, one isolated `gpt-5.6-sol` xhigh blind judge, and at most two
  documented pre-action infrastructure replacements; 15 subscription sessions maximum.
- Isolation: disposable workspaces and config homes, identical repository commit and permissions,
  no ambient instructions/memory/skills/plugins/MCP, and no network unless one frozen task requires
  it.
- Evidence: automated correctness first, anonymous general-quality judgment second.

The practical gate requires 12 valid cells, no neutral-control regression or false activation,
complete instruction provenance, projection size at or below 1,500 bytes, and either a diagnostic
correctness advantage over Base or equal correctness with a measured longitudinal
correction/maintenance advantage. Prior strong-static experiments remain a separate ceiling
comparison and are not mixed statistically with this result.

## Invite-only alpha

The alpha keeps raw interaction content encrypted and local with a 30-day default retention
period. It exports only a signed sanitized aggregate. Review mode, exact scope, future-use
confirmation, provenance, revocation, and undo remain mandatory. Automatic promotion and presets
remain disabled.

The implemented product commands are `temper status`, `temper poll`, `temper review`,
`temper approve`, `temper reject`, `temper adaptations`, `temper run`, `temper feedback`,
`temper undo`, `temper activity`, `temper history`, `temper health`, `temper gate`, and `temper
advanced`. The default surface is consumer-oriented; exact IDs, digests, provenance, research-gate
metrics, and raw-free diagnostics remain available through the advanced views. The existing research CLI
remains available for initialization, watcher installation, project registration, and compatibility
until those setup flows receive an alpha-safe wrapper. `temper init` and a signed sanitized
`temper export-report` are therefore explicit Phase 5 freeze gaps, not undocumented commands. A
compact terminal inbox and generic native notification are sufficient for the first alpha; no web
UI is required.

Planning a public preview requires at least three active participants, 100 eligible interactions
across 14 days, 30 completed rated activations, zero high-severity incidents, false learning at or
below 5%, missed learning at or below 10%, exact activation at or above 85%, false activation at or
below 5%, helped outcomes exceeding hurt outcomes, at least 80% correction-key non-repetition
after activation, compact projections within budget, and median review burden no greater than two
minutes per day.
