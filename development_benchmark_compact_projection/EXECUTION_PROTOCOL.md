## Information separation

The v1.13 remediation reviewer receives the same eligible evidence as construction and only the
frozen v1.11 profile drafts, but no projection, compiled pack, tasks, mappings, scores, target
outputs, or prior target outputs. Construction does not receive task fixtures or scorer outcomes.
Targets receive one blinded condition and the identical shared envelope. The judge receives the
exact allowed-path and neutral execution constraints but no condition identity.

The deferred external-validation study is outside this workspace and outside this protocol.

## Success gate

The future comparison passes only if:

- all six target records are valid;
- compiled has no objective, diagnostic, or neutral regression;
- false activation and scope leakage are both zero;
- compact target material is no larger than the independently maintained profile unless a frozen
  semantic-necessity record explains the difference;
- every clause retains external provenance and revocation guarantees; and
- no AI preference is used to rescue a failed primary gate.

Parity in behavior and size is meaningful only because the compiler separately retains automatic
updating, governance, portability, and rollback. Governance alone is not a pass when target
quality or discipline regresses.

## Stopping and tuning

Renderer version 1.1.0 and the v1.13 tasks are immutable once the remediation reviewer or any
target session starts. Observed live outputs may inform a later version and entirely new tasks,
but they may not tune and rerun this version against the same tasks. Stop immediately on
private-plan drift, condition leakage, workspace escape, missing receipt, API-key use,
external-validation access, or scorer substitution.
